# vehicle — cars, motorcycles, anything with a plate

Use for car-lot inventory, "spotted on the street" sales posts, before/after detailing shots.

## Template

> Professional automotive photography retouch of this exact photo. The vehicle is {SUBJECT — e.g. "a white 2019 Honda Civic sedan, three-quarter front view"}. Keep the vehicle completely unchanged — same body, same paint color, same wheels, same trim and badges, same visible condition including any dents or wear (this is a truthful sales photo). Blur the license plate. Re-light as a professional dealership shot: {LIGHTING — pick one: "late golden-hour sunlight giving clean warm reflections along the body lines" / "bright overcast studio-like light with soft even reflections"}. {BACKGROUND — pick one: "Clean up the background into an uncluttered upscale street scene, softly blurred" / "Place the car in a minimalist showroom environment with a polished floor"}. Remove distracting elements from the background (bins, poles, other cars' halves), fix {FLAWS — e.g. "the underexposed shadow side and the tilted framing"}, keep reflections on the paint physically plausible. Photorealistic, high-end automotive marketing quality. No added text, no watermark, no bodywork "repairs".

## Notes

- Never change paint color or "fix" body damage — that's misrepresentation, not polish.
- Keep the original camera angle; automotive models love to invent a new one.
- If people are reflected in the paint or visible in windows, remove or blur them.
