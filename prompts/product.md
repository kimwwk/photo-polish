# product — inventory, retail items, things for sale

Use for merchandise: electronics, furniture, clothing, tools, collectibles, bulk inventory items.

## Template

> Professional e-commerce product photography retouch of this exact photo. The product is {SUBJECT — e.g. "a brown leather office chair"}. Keep the product completely unchanged — same condition, same color, same details, including any visible wear, scratches or imperfections (this is a truthful listing photo). Isolate the product from the cluttered surroundings and place it on {BACKGROUND — pick one: "a seamless white studio background" / "a soft neutral gray gradient studio background"} with a natural soft shadow beneath it grounding it. Light it like a studio softbox setup: even, diffused lighting, no harsh reflections, true-to-life colors. Fix {FLAWS — e.g. "the dim lighting and the busy garage background"}. Crisp focus across the whole product, photorealistic, clean commercial catalog quality. No added text, no watermark, no props that aren't in the original.

## Notes

- Seamless white = marketplace listings (Amazon/eBay style); gray gradient = premium look for social. Default to white for "selling it" contexts.
- For reflective items (glass, metal, screens) add: "control the reflections so no photographer or phone is visible in the surface".
- Multiple items in frame: keep the count and arrangement identical — never let the model tidy up the quantity.
