# jewelry — rings, necklaces, earrings, watches, loose stones

Use when the subject is a piece of jewelry or a watch, shot for a listing, catalog, or social post.

## Template

> Professional jewelry product photography retouch of this exact photo. The piece is {SUBJECT — e.g. "a yellow-gold ring with a single round-cut diamond"}. Keep the piece completely unchanged — same metal color and finish, same stones (same count, cut, color and clarity as visible), same engravings and hallmarks, including any visible wear or patina (this is a truthful listing photo). Keep the exact original camera angle and framing. Isolate it from the surroundings and place it on {BACKGROUND — pick one: "a seamless pure white studio background (RGB 255, 255, 255) with a subtle, natural contact shadow beneath the piece grounding it" / "deep black velvet with a soft focused spotlight" / "a soft neutral gray gradient"}. Light it like a professional jewelry lightbox: fully diffused illumination with controlled specular highlights, crisp clean sparkle on the facets without blown-out hotspots, and no photographer, phone or window reflected in the metal or stones. Correct any lens or perspective distortion, and fix {FLAWS — e.g. "the yellow indoor color cast and the soft focus"}. Macro-level sharpness across the whole piece, true-to-life metal tone — do not shift gold, rose gold or silver toward each other. Photorealistic, luxury catalog quality. No added text, no watermark, no artificial sparkle effects or lens flares that misrepresent the stones.

## Notes

- Seamless white = marketplace listings; black velvet = luxury/social look. Default to white for "selling it" contexts.
- Metal tone drift is the classic failure — if the input is ambiguous, state the metal explicitly in {SUBJECT}.
- For watches: keep the dial time, logo and text exactly as shot; specify "no warping of the dial text".
- Stones are the product: never let the model enlarge, multiply or "clarify" them (an opaque or crackled stone stays opaque and crackled).
- Very small or blurry inputs: the model re-renders detail it can't see — add "do not invent engravings, markings or surface texture that are not visible in the original" to the prompt.
