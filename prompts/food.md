## Notes

- **Identity = the food.** Components, their character and portions must survive. The vessel, plating arrangement, camera angle and setting are *presentation* — showcase mode restyles them freely (that's where the wow lives).
- Delivery-app style = keep the packaging, brighter/cleaner scene; dine-in style = replate + moody restaurant scene. Infer from the user's wording; default to the dine-in restyle.
- If the user asks for something subtler ("natural", "keep my angle", "keep the box"), honor it — swap in the keep-packaging / keep-angle branches and soften the lighting to "soft directional key light, gentle fill, natural appetizing highlights".
- **Truthful mode** (photo must show the food as-is): keep the original packaging, angle and condition — no replating, no steam, "do not add, remove, reshape or improve any food item".
