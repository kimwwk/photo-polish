# general — everything else

Fallback for interiors, storefronts, workspaces, pets, landscapes, people at work (with their consent).

## Template

> Professional photography retouch of this exact photo. The subject is {SUBJECT — e.g. "a barbershop interior with two styling chairs"}. Keep the scene and every subject unchanged — same objects, same layout, same people (if any). Improve it like a professional photographer's edit: balanced natural-looking lighting that opens up the shadows without looking HDR-fake, corrected white balance, {FLAWS — e.g. "fix the mixed fluorescent color cast and the slight blur"}, gently declutter only trash-level distractions {DISTRACTIONS — e.g. "the cable on the floor"} if removing them doesn't change what the place honestly looks like. Natural colors with tasteful contrast, crisp detail, straight verticals. Photorealistic, gallery-grade. No added text, no watermark, no beauty-filter look on faces.

## Notes

- Faces: enhancement only (exposure/sharpness) — never reshape features or skin-smooth into a filter look.
- Storefront/interior shots sell trust: prioritize "clean and honest" over "dramatic".
- If the photo is severely blurred or tiny, say so — an edit model can't recover what isn't there, and pretending otherwise produces mush.
